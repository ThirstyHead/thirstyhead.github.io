Projects

A curated collection of educational platforms, architectural demonstrations, and informational resources designed and maintained by Scott Davis outside of ThirstyHead.

These properties serve as living laboratories for modern web development, exploring how semantic standards, evidence-based architecture, and visual simplicity can be leveraged to build a web that is radically accessible for everyone.

  • simpleaccess.io

    SimpleAccess

    SimpleAccess is an educational hub dedicated to demystifying digital accessibility. Grounded in the philosophy that accessibility is fundamentally about "Sight, Sound, and Touch," the platform breaks down complex Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) into manageable, evidence-based architectural patterns.

    Rather than treating accessibility as an afterthought or a tedious compliance checklist, SimpleAccess provides software engineers, designers, and project managers with the practical tools needed to build digital environments that are inherently inclusive from the ground up—proving that accessible software is simply better software.

    Visit SimpleAccess.io
  • a11yview.com

    A11yView

    A11yView is an interactive web architecture demonstration that allows developers to experience the web through the lens of assistive technology. It visualizes exactly how semantic HTML, ARIA attributes, and accessible design patterns translate into real-world screen reader output and keyboard navigation constraints.

    By bridging the gap between raw markup and human perception, A11yView challenges the industry to move beyond visual aesthetics. It serves as a vital tool for understanding how structural decisions made in the codebase directly impact the cognitive load and navigational experience of end users who rely on assistive devices.

    Visit A11yView.com
